About the clinic

A family clinic that has grown up with Gandhibagh.

Our clinic opened more than fifty years ago, and I grew up around it. I took my dental degree in Switzerland and chose to bring that training back home to Gandhibagh, because this is where our patients are.

Many patients today were first brought here by their parents. Now my daughter, Dr. Pooja, works beside me, so the same care passes to the next generation.

We follow one rule: the rule of good dentistry, with no partiality. A first-time patient and a fifty-year-old one get the same attention.

  • Fully sanitized, clean treatment room
  • Separate, comfortable area for waiting patients
  • Modern digital X-ray for clear diagnosis
  • Swiss-trained doctor with 50+ years of clinic experience
  • Rules followed, no partiality
